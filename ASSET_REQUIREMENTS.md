## How To Capture

- Use real screenshots from your phone or from Play Store/App Store listing media.
- Capture the most representative screens: home/dashboard, primary workflow, and result/export/detail screen.
- Avoid screenshots with personal data, private client records, test credentials, or notification clutter.
- Use light or dark app screenshots consistently per app if possible.
